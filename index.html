01 · Where it started

It began with one photo, taken apart.

In 2018 I spent whole nights breaking a single portrait into hundreds of fragments in Photoshop, until the person looked like they were dissolving into the air. That's what you're watching behind this text. The same curiosity about how images change has carried me from design into engineering.

02 · What I'm building

Become any character, live.

SmokeScreen lets streamers, content creators and gamers step into a completely different character using an ordinary webcam. No motion-capture rig, no green screen, no editing. Try switching characters:

20,000+people have tried it
Apr 2026launched, the month I graduated
~70%are new to AI, so I keep it simple

What it's teaching me

  • Real users are the best teachers. Their feedback shapes every release.
  • Build for someone who has never used AI before, not for the expert.
  • Never depend on a single supplier. When ours shut our account, we switched without users noticing.
